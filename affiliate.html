A SHARED PURPOSE

Be part of a growing learning movement.

TEXLearn brings together curated learning resources that encourage practical knowledge, entrepreneurial thinking, financial literacy and personal growth.

As a partner, you can help introduce these resources to your community while building an earning opportunity around the recommendations you make.

WHO CAN PARTNER?

There is more than one way to grow with us.

Whether you are part of a campus community or have an audience of your own, there may be a place for you in the TEXLearn partner network.

Student Ambassadors

Introduce TEXLearn to fellow students and campus communities through your personal networks, social channels and QR code.

Independent Affiliates

Recommend TEXLearn to people who may benefit from practical learning resources and earn on eligible purchases you refer.

REWARDS FOR YOUR IMPACT

Your referrals can create value for you and for learners.

Partners earn commission on eligible purchases successfully referred through their assigned link or QR code. Your commission rate, attribution period, validation rules and payout schedule will be shared when your application is approved.

Commissions are based on verified payments, subject to the programme terms. Refunded, cancelled or fraudulent transactions are not eligible.
